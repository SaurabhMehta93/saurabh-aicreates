Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Send us a DM on Instagram @saurabh.aicreates with the word "STORY", your function type and date. We will reply with the next steps.

What details do I need to give?
  • Names (bride & groom / birthday person / family)
  • Function date, time and venue
  • Language: Hindi, Gujarati or English
  • Photos (optional – only if you want your look in the video)
  • Any theme or colour you like
How long does delivery take?

Image invitation: 1–2 days. Video invitation: 3–5 days. Need it faster? Express delivery in 24 hours is available for +30%.

How does payment work?

50% advance to start the work. We send a watermarked preview. After you approve it, pay the balance and we send the final file without watermark. Payment by UPI.

How many changes can I ask for?

2 free revisions are included (names, date, venue, text, small changes). Extra changes after that cost ₹99 each. A completely new idea or theme after approval is charged as a new order.

Is the price based on video length?

Yes. Longer videos need more scenes, so they cost more. See all prices on the pricing section.

Do I have to send my photos?

No. We can create the full invitation with AI characters. If you want the couple or family to look like you, send 2–4 clear photos.

Will the people in the video look exactly like us?

AI gives a close, artistic look – not a 100% exact copy of a real photo. Faces may look slightly different between scenes. We always show a preview before final delivery.

In which format will I get the file?

Video: MP4 (vertical 9:16, ready for WhatsApp status and Instagram). Image: JPG / PNG. Delivered on WhatsApp or Google Drive link.

Can you write text in Gujarati or Hindi?

Yes. Hindi, Gujarati and English are available. An extra language version of the same video costs ₹199.

Can I get a refund?

The advance is not refundable once work has started, because time and AI tools are already used. If we cannot deliver, we refund your advance in full.

Will you post my video on your page?

Only if you allow it. We always ask before sharing any client video on Instagram or this website. See our Privacy Policy.

What other videos do you make?

Birthday and anniversary films, family story films, devotional videos and promo reels for businesses. DM us your idea.
