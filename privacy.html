Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Saurabh AI Creates ("we", "us") makes AI invitation images and videos. This policy explains what information we collect when you order, how we use it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Information we collect

  • Your name and Instagram / WhatsApp contact
  • Event details: names, date, time, venue, family names
  • Photos you choose to send (optional)
  • Payment confirmation (we do not store card or bank details)

2. How we use it

  • Only to create and deliver your invitation or video
  • To contact you about your order
  • We never sell or share your details for marketing

3. AI tools

To create your content we use third-party AI tools (for example ChatGPT and Google Flow). Your text and photos may be processed by these tools only to generate your order. Their own privacy policies also apply.

4. Showing your work

We will not post your video or photos on Instagram, YouTube or this website without your permission. If you allow it, we may remove names or personal details before sharing. You can ask us to remove a shared post at any time.

5. How long we keep your files

  • Your original photos are deleted within 30 days after final delivery
  • The final video may be kept up to 90 days so we can resend it if needed
  • You can ask us to delete everything sooner – just DM us

6. Preview links

Preview videos are watermarked. Any preview link we share is for you only – please do not forward it if you want the video to stay private.

7. Children

Photos of children are used only when sent by a parent or guardian, and only for that order.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we have about you at any time.

9. Contact

For any privacy question or request, DM us on Instagram @saurabh.aicreates.
